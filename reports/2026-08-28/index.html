August traffic has already surpassed July, reaching 25,830 sessions compared to July's 25,291 total, with three days still remaining in the month. The site continues to grow month-over-month.

Organic Search remains the dominant traffic driver at 56% of all sessions with a 65.55% engagement rate, consistent with July. The SEO content strategy continues to compound, bringing in high-quality visitors who engage deeply with CPI content.

Paid Search is the highest-quality channel on the site. Visitors from Google Ads engage at 65.26%, spend 1m 20s per session, and trigger 13.27 events per visit (up from 12.56 in July). These are intent-driven visitors actively exploring services and taking action.

AI Assistant traffic has grown to 122 sessions, up 34% from July's 91. With a 60.66% engagement rate, CPI content continues to surface in AI-powered search tools, and those visitors engage more deeply than most other channels.

Device Insights

Mobile captures 92.6% of ad spend ($1,217) and drives nearly 100% of conversions (19 of 19). Desktop accounts for 7.1% of spend but 36.2% of clicks (192), suggesting desktop users click through from ads but are less likely to convert. This is consistent with a pattern where mobile users searching for therapy services are more likely to take immediate action (call or submit a form) from their phone.

Work Completed

  • Created SEO-optimized blog content with schema markup and custom imagery.

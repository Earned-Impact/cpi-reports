Through 22 days, Google Ads has delivered 2 conversions at $209.50 each on $419 in total spend. For context, September's full-month spend was $856 through 24 days, so October is pacing lower overall.

The biggest development this month is that Child & Adolescent Therapy is now actively spending at $108.53 with 101 clicks. This campaign was essentially dormant as of the last report ($0.01 spend). The Child Therapy Focus ad group increased spend by 222% compared to the prior period, indicating the campaign has been relaunched.

Search - Adult Therapy Services accounts for $310.16 in spend with 378 clicks and both conversions. Within this campaign, the High Intent Keywords ad group decreased spend by 46.26% ($183.09 less than the prior period), while Condition-Specific Keywords decreased 19.09% ($22.99 less).

Mobile continues to drive 100% of conversions, consistent with the pattern seen all year. Mobile accounts for 56.6% of clicks and 75.6% of ad spend. Computers represent 42.6% of clicks but 23.7% of spend, suggesting lower CPCs on desktop.

SEO Roadmap

  • URL consolidation (30-day priority): Redirect /double-bind-2/ to /double-bind/ and /emotional-masochism/ to /masochistic-character/ to resolve keyword cannibalization. These splits are currently fragmenting roughly 620 clicks per month.
  • Title and snippet rewrites for five high-impression, low-click queries: "masochist meaning" (132K impressions, 35 clicks), "neurotic" (102K impressions, 57 clicks), "delusional," "paranoid," and "neuroticism."
  • Chicago local markup: Add LocalBusiness and MedicalClinic schema to /adult-clinical-services/, /therapists/, and /programs-of-study/. Add GeoCoordinates site-wide.
  • Google Business Profile: CPI currently has 4 reviews (4.0 to 5.0 stars) versus a competitor with 15 reviews (4.9 stars). Setting up a post-intake review request flow would close this gap.
  • GA4 key event setup (90-day): Configure a consultation/program-inquiry key event for organic traffic. Currently only "purchase" is configured and records zero events.

Next Steps

  • Finalize conversion tracking so the remaining campaigns (Life Transitions, Holiday, Seasonal Depression) can go live. This is the primary blocker for expanding paid reach.
  • Launch Life Transitions campaign as the next priority once tracking is confirmed.
  • Holiday and Seasonal Depression campaigns are targeting November launch windows. Tracking needs to be resolved before then to stay on schedule.

  • Continue new content publishing. September's 11 new pages generated 706 clicks at 3x the site average CTR, validating the content strategy.

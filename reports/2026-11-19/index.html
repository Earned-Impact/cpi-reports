Recently Delivered

Barr-Harris Contact Audit

A digital audit of Barr-Harris contact information across the web found 14 websites showing outdated details (wrong phone, address, or name). The root cause: the old website (barrharris.org) is still live with 2018 info, and nearly every wrong listing copied from it. The old site has also been compromised with hidden spam links.

Four sites are under CPI's direct control and can be corrected immediately. Ten require outreach to third-party directories. The full audit with specific fix instructions for each listing is available at the link below.

Archival Audio Paywall Scoping

A technical scoping report was delivered for selling access to CPI's audio archive from the existing WordPress site. The recommended approach uses MemberPress for access control and Cloudflare R2 for private audio storage, with year one platform costs of about $360. No technical blockers were found. Five decisions from CPI (archive size, pricing model, stream vs. download) will finalize the build and a fixed quote.

Quarterly Audit: Three-Week Check-In

The account audit implemented on September 3 has had three weeks to settle in. The negative keyword additions (blocking "mental hospital," "psych ward," and similar irrelevant searches) and the 17 paused duplicate keywords are reducing wasted spend. The overall click-through rate has improved from 7.94% in the first 10 days to 9.92% for the month so far, which suggests the cleanup is working as intended.

The RSA expansion (additional ad variations per ad group) remains in progress and will provide further optimization gains once live.

The August SEO report is now complete and shows strong continued growth. Organic sessions reached 16,882, up 5.2% month-over-month and up 169% compared to August 2025. The site generated 14,271 search clicks, roughly 3x the year-ago level.

Form submissions surged 65.7%, from 99 in July to 164 in August. File downloads also increased 56.6% (from 320 to 501). These engagement metrics indicate that the content strategy is not only bringing more visitors but converting them into prospective patients and program applicants.

Ten new articles were published in August, and 13 new URLs generated 1,091 clicks in their first month. Top-performing new pages: /malignant-narcissism (360 clicks at position 5.8), borderline personality organization (246 clicks at position 4.2), and /donald-winnicott (230 clicks at position 4.3).

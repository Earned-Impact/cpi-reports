Next Steps

1. Increase Desktop Bid Adjustments

Desktop users only receive 7% of ad spend but deliver 12.5% of conversions, converting at nearly 2x the rate of mobile. We plan to increase the desktop bid adjustment to capture more of these high-intent searchers and improve overall conversion efficiency.

2. Expand Child & Adolescent Campaign

The Child & Adolescent campaign is showing strong momentum: parent-focused keywords up 538% and teen keywords up 175%. We will expand keyword coverage in this area and explore creating a dedicated landing page for child and adolescent services to improve conversion from these clicks.

3. Branded Search Keyword Optimization

"Chicago psychoanalytic institute" is driving 96 clicks at a 27.35% CTR, the strongest performing keyword by far. We will review the branded keyword strategy to ensure we are capturing all relevant branded and near-branded search variations at the lowest possible cost.

4. SEO Alignment for Q3

With the paid campaigns driving strong traffic to therapy-related terms, we want to align the organic SEO strategy to complement these efforts. Specifically, we will review page content and metadata for the adult therapy and child/adolescent service pages to improve organic rankings alongside the paid campaigns.

5. Barr-Harris Go-to-Market Brief

We are preparing a go-to-market brief for Barr-Harris paid ads, targeting a late July launch. This will include audience strategy, keyword research, ad creative direction, and budget recommendations. More details to follow as we finalize the plan.

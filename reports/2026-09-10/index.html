Google Ads Account Audit — Completed

Quarterly Audit  |  Implemented September 3, 2026

The quarterly Google Ads account audit has been completed and implemented. This was the deep-dive review previewed in the August report. Here is a summary of the changes made:

Blocked Irrelevant Searches

People searching for things like "mental hospital," "psych ward," or "university of chicago" were seeing CPI's ads and sometimes clicking on them — even though they weren't looking for therapy. We blocked these terms so those searches no longer trigger ads. Think of it like putting up a "wrong door" sign — it stops the wrong visitors from coming in and using up the ad budget.

Terms blocked:

  • "mental hospitals" / "mental hospital"
  • "mental institution" / "mental institutions"
  • "psych ward" / "psych wards"
  • "scott harms rose"
  • "university of chicago"

Cleaned Up Duplicate Keywords

Paused 17 keywords that overlapped with other keywords already in the account. When multiple keywords cover the same search, they compete against each other — like bidding against yourself at an auction. Removing the duplicates lets the remaining keywords work more efficiently with the same budget.

Standardized Audience Settings

As part of the audit, we reviewed how audience settings were configured across ad groups. We updated two ad groups to use "Observation" mode, which is the best practice when you're not targeting a specific audience list. This means the ads continue to show to everyone searching relevant terms, and we can now also track which types of people are most likely to click — giving us better data to optimize with over time.

More Ad Variations — Approved, Coming Soon

Right now each ad group has only one version of its ad. We've been approved to create additional versions (called Responsive Search Ads). This gives Google more headlines and descriptions to mix and match, so it can show the most effective combination to each person. It's like A/B testing multiple versions of a flyer to see which one gets the most responses.

Optimization scores currently sit at 59.4% (Adult Therapy) and 61.8% (Child & Adolescent). These are similar to pre-audit levels, which is expected — the changes were just implemented on September 3, and it typically takes 2–4 weeks for Google to fully adjust. We expect improvements in the next report, especially once the new ad variations are also live.

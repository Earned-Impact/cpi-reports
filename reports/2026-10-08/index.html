What's missing: The conversion tracking matrix for CPI does not yet have business actions mapped (lead form submissions, appointment bookings, phone calls). Until these are configured as GA4 key events and imported into Google Ads, the 1 conversion reported in Google Ads may not reflect the full picture of lead generation.

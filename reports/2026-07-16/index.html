SEO Is the Top Driver of Inquiry-Ready Traffic

Organic search delivers 83% of all engaged sessions on the site (9,810 of 11,811 total). These visitors engage at a 64.03% rate, spend over a minute per session, and trigger 6+ events per visit. By every quality metric, organic search is the primary channel putting potential clients in front of the inquiry form.

In the past 12 months, the SEO program has tripled organic sessions (from 4,939 to 14,919), multiplied search impressions 12x (from 267K to 3.16M), and improved average search position from 7.3 to 6.9. A slight month-over-month dip (sessions down 9.7%) is consistent with typical summer seasonality and does not change the year-over-year trajectory.

In the Pipeline

Barr-Harris Go-to-Market Campaign (Paid Ads)

We are developing a paid ads campaign to increase visibility for the Barr-Harris Center, CPI's child and adolescent therapy program. The campaign will build on the momentum of the existing Child & Adolescent search campaign, which has already produced one conversion this period at $25.05 in spend.

The go-to-market plan will include audience targeting strategy, keyword research focused on parent-intent search terms, ad creative direction, and budget recommendations. Given the prior success of parent-focused keywords (which saw strong growth before this period's spend dip), there is a solid foundation to build on.

We will share the full brief for review before launching. Target launch: fall 2026.

Next Steps

1. Increase Desktop Bid Adjustments

Desktop users receive only 6.3% of ad spend but deliver 12.5% of conversions and capture 40.8% of all clicks. We plan to increase desktop bid adjustments to capture more of these high-converting searchers.

2. Rebuild Child & Adolescent Spend

The Child & Adolescent campaign saw the steepest declines this period, with teen keywords down 82% and child therapy keywords down 54%. We will work to restore spend levels in these ad groups to recapture the strong growth trajectory from prior periods.

3. SEO: Push Page-Two Keywords to Page One

Several high-volume keywords ("masochist meaning" at 144K impressions, "schizoid personality disorder" at 34K, "cyclothymia" at 30K) are sitting in positions 9 to 11. Title and meta description rewrites for these pages could move them onto page one and unlock significant additional traffic.

4. Launch Barr-Harris Paid Ads Campaign

The go-to-market brief for Barr-Harris paid ads is in development, targeting a fall launch. We will share the plan for review before going live.

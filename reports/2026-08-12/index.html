This is a shift from July, where desktop converted at more than double the mobile rate. With only 11 days of data, this will likely even out as the month progresses.

In the Pipeline

Barr-Harris Go-to-Market Campaign (Paid Ads)

The Barr-Harris paid ads campaign remains on track for a fall launch (September/October 2026). The plan is to start with an evergreen Google Ads campaign at a smaller budget ($250 to $500 per month, allocated from the existing ad spend bucket) focused on general awareness and directing traffic to the Barr-Harris website.

Because CPI currently has more capacity for adult services than child-specific clinicians, the initial campaign will target a broader audience rather than a child-grief-only focus. As the Barr-Harris program builds clinician capacity (expected spring 2027), a child-focused campaign layer will be added.

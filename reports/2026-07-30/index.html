Organic Search continues to drive the majority of traffic, accounting for 59% of all sessions with a strong 63.33% engagement rate and over a minute of average session time.

Paid Search remains the highest-quality traffic source on the site. Paid visitors engage at 67.31%, spend 1m 25s per session, and trigger 12.56 events per visit. That is more than double the site average and a clear signal that these are intent-driven visitors ready to take action.

Budget Context

Google Ads spend for July came in below the normal monthly budget due to a payment processing disruption early in the month. The ad team proactively lowered budgets while the issue was being resolved to prevent Google from dumping accumulated spend into a short window. Budgets have now been fully restored, and the campaigns are ramping back to normal levels.

Despite the reduced budget, the campaigns delivered 15 conversions for the month. The Adult Therapy campaign drove 12 of those, reflecting strong demand for adult clinical services. This aligns well with CPI's current capacity, which has more availability on the adult side.

The Child & Adolescent campaign contributed 3 conversions at $218 in spend. As the Barr-Harris program builds clinician capacity (expected spring 2027), paid ads will be ready to scale alongside it.

Spend Recovery vs. June

All ad groups saw spend increases compared to June, reflecting the budget recovery after the payment disruption:

  • Parent-Focused Keywords: +$67.55 (+295.8%)
  • Condition-Specific Keywords: +$100.45 (+96.5%)
  • Adolescent/Teen Keywords: +$45.90 (+97.7%)
  • Child Therapy Focus: +$17.34 (+98.7%)
  • High Intent Keywords: +$83.17 (+23.7%)

These increases reflect budgets returning to normal, not a spike in cost. As the campaigns run at full budget for a complete month, both spend and conversion volume should continue to climb.

Device & Demographic Insights

94.0% of ad spend goes to mobile devices, which drive 86.7% of conversions (13 of 15). Desktop accounts for only 5.9% of spend but 13.3% of conversions and 36.1% of all clicks. Desktop users continue to convert at more than double the rate of mobile, reinforcing the case for increased desktop bid adjustments.

Strongest demographic reach: females ages 25 to 44 (25.3% of known impressions), with solid male representation across all age groups. Females account for 54.9% of impressions and males 45.1%.

Peak Search Days

Highest impression volume falls on Monday through Thursday, with Monday leading at 875 impressions. Weekend activity is lighter, consistent with prior months.

In the Pipeline

Barr-Harris Go-to-Market Campaign (Paid Ads)

The Barr-Harris paid ads campaign is on track for a fall launch (September/October 2026). The plan is to start with an evergreen Google Ads campaign at a smaller budget ($250 to $500 per month, allocated from the existing ad spend bucket) focused on general awareness and directing traffic to the Barr-Harris website.

Because CPI currently has more capacity for adult services than child-specific clinicians, the initial campaign will target a broader audience rather than a child-grief-only focus. As the Barr-Harris program builds clinician capacity (expected spring 2027), a child-focused campaign layer will be added.

Running the campaign at a lower budget through the fall and winter allows Google to learn and optimize targeting before scaling. The draft go-to-market brief will be shared for review before launch.

Archives Digitization Project

The team discussed the archives digitization project with John Leonard. This would involve building the infrastructure on the CPI website to host archived content, potentially with a paywall. CPI will handle the bulk of the content upload work. This is not urgent and is being considered as a fall project, with scoping to follow.

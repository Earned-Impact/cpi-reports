Conversions doubled from 2 to 4 compared to last period. Two of the four campaigns are now converting: Adult Therapy Services produced 3 conversions at $95.73 each, and Barr-Harris (children's program) delivered its first conversion in its inaugural reporting period.

Two new campaigns went live since the last report. Barr-Harris and Transitional Life Stages (ages 20 to 30) are both running and generating clicks. Life Stages has not yet converted but is still in its early weeks with 76 clicks across its Family Dynamics & Relationships ad group.

Child & Adolescent Therapy generated 276 clicks (up from 101 last period) but has not yet converted. With that volume of traffic, this campaign is worth watching for conversion tracking issues or landing page optimization opportunities.

Mobile continues to drive 100% of conversions, consistent with the pattern seen all year. Desktop generates 30% of clicks but zero conversions, suggesting the mobile landing page experience may be a key factor in what makes visitors convert.

Search Terms to Watch

The new campaigns are pulling in relevant new keyword territory: "death of a loved one" (47 clicks, Barr-Harris) and "therapy for relationship patterns" (43 clicks, Life Stages). The branded term "Chicago psychoanalytic institute" remains the highest-volume keyword with 149 clicks at a 36.17% CTR.

A few competitor or unrelated terms are triggering ads: "trilogy chicago," "nocd," "sage therapy chicago," and "emdr chicago." Adding these as negative keywords would reduce wasted clicks.

SEO Roadmap

  • URL consolidation: Redirect /double-bind-2/ to /double-bind/ and /emotional-masochism/ to /masochistic-character/ to resolve keyword cannibalization.
  • Title and snippet rewrites for high-impression, low-click queries: "masochist meaning," "neurotic," "delusional," "paranoid," and "neuroticism."
  • Chicago local markup: Add LocalBusiness and MedicalClinic schema to /adult-clinical-services/, /therapists/, and /programs-of-study/.
  • GA4 key event setup (priority): Configure intake/consultation form submissions as a key event. This is the missing link between ad campaigns and actual client inquiries. Once set up, we can report which campaigns and keywords are driving real form fills, not just clicks.

Next Steps

  • Set up form submission tracking in GA4. This is the top priority. Once intake/consultation forms are configured as key events, we can connect ad clicks directly to real inquiries and report on which campaigns drive actual form fills.
  • Launch Holiday campaign around Thanksgiving week. New Year campaign goes live between Christmas and NYE. Seasonal Depression is scheduled for late January/early February.
  • Monitor new campaign conversions. Barr-Harris and Transitional Life Stages are in their first reporting period. Barr-Harris already has 1 conversion. Both need a few more weeks of data before optimization.
  • Add negative keywords. "Trilogy chicago," "nocd," "sage therapy chicago," and "emdr chicago" are triggering CPI ads and wasting clicks.
  • Consolidate split URLs. /double-bind/ and /emotional-masochism/ redirects remain the top SEO priority.
